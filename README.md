# haven-bounce-back
this is a game where you drive a twingo featuring a variable throttle through three different lanes littered with slimes and cones.
if you hit a slime, you fly to heaven. but you lose the cumulative points you got through the game. avoid them.
if you hit a cone, you go to hell, not because you hit a cone, but because you don't meet timed quota of points.
so you must dodge. lower and increase your throttle using <down> and <up>, and you change lanes using <left> and <right>.
this is the only way you can survive. dont go too slow or go to hell. dont get too hot on your britches or go to heaven. you'll lose your points either way. <b>survive.</b>
